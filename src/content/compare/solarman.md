## Solarman is the pipe, PVBiz sits on top

Many inverters, including a large share of Deye and other hybrid inverters in South Africa, report through a Solarman data logger. The logger uploads to the Solarman cloud, and the Solarman Smart or Solarman Business app shows the result.

PVBiz reads from that same cloud. You share the plant with the PVBiz account from the Sharing menu in Solarman, and PVBiz pulls solar, load, battery and grid figures about every five minutes. Installers can add a Solarman login instead and choose which plants to sync. Nothing on the logger or the inverter changes.

## Where PVBiz adds something

**Alerts on your terms.** Solarman sends the logger's alarms by push, email or SMS. PVBiz runs its own rules: the battery level you care about, a grid loss or restore, no data for a period, or a threshold on any metric it reads. Each rule goes to the channels you choose, including Telegram and WhatsApp.

**More than Solarman.** A plant on Solarman and another on Sunsynk, Solis or Huawei FusionSolar sit in one PVBiz subscription with one set of rules.

**Installer fleets.** On the PVBiz Installer Platform a Solarman fleet sits next to every other brand you install, with fleet alerts, work orders, Eskom and municipal tariff savings and branded client reports on a schedule.

## Where Solarman is enough

If every plant you look after has a Solarman logger, Solarman Business already gives you a fleet dashboard, report templates and work orders, and Solarman Smart is free for homeowners.
