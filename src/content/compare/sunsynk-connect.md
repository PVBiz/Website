## Two apps, one data source

Sunsynk inverters report through a data logger to the Sunsynk cloud. Sunsynk Connect is the app Sunsynk builds on top of that cloud, and it is where you change settings, view your plant and share it with your installer.

PVBiz reads from the same cloud. You share the plant with PVBiz, or add your own Sunsynk login, and PVBiz starts pulling your power flow, battery, grid and inverter figures about every five minutes. Nothing is installed and nothing on the inverter changes unless you give PVBiz Manager permission and then ask it to change a setting.

So this is rarely an either-or choice. Sunsynk Connect stays the maker's app. PVBiz adds what it does not cover.

## Where PVBiz adds something

**Alerts you control.** Sunsynk Connect notifies you about grid loss, faults and power loss. PVBiz covers those, and lets you set your own thresholds: a battery level that matters to you, an inverter temperature, essential load, or no data arriving for a period you choose. Each rule goes to the channels you pick, including Telegram and WhatsApp.

**More than one brand.** A Sunsynk at home and a Deye or Solis somewhere else sit together in one PVBiz subscription, with one set of rules.

**Planning ahead.** A 7-day production forecast from local weather helps you plan the geyser, the pool pump or charging around the sun.

**Schedules and automation on Sunsynk.** With Manager permission, PVBiz can change force charge, work mode, export and the battery timer, on a schedule or when a condition is met.

## Where Sunsynk Connect is enough

If you have one Sunsynk system and the built-in notifications tell you what you need, Sunsynk Connect does the job at no cost. Its PDF reports cover a day, week, month, year or custom period, and installers with Installer User Level access can download minute-level operating data in Excel.
